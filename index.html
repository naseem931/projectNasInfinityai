Privacy Notice & Legal Disclaimer

NasInfinity AI v2.0.0 • Local-First, Zero-Telemetry Architecture

1. Local-First Client Architecture (Zero Remote Logging)

The host web server (server.js) collects no tracking cookies, zero analytics telemetry, and maintains zero database logs of your prompts or personal data.

Your conversation history, profile memory, theme preference, and workspace settings stay inside your device's localStorage.

2. Third-Party AI Services & Network Transmission

  • Online First AI: Proxied via public text endpoints (Pollinations.ai).
  • Google Gemini API: Prompts & attachments go directly to Google servers using your personal key (Terms).
  • OpenAI API: Prompts & attachments go directly to OpenAI using your personal key (Terms).
  • Wikipedia REST API: Factual summaries retrieved under CC BY-SA 4.0.

3. API Keys & Data Erasure

Personal API keys are saved solely inside your browser storage and never logged by our server. You can purge all chats anytime via Clear in the sidebar or say "Reset memory".
