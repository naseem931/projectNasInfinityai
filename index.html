Privacy Notice & Legal Disclaimer

NasInfinity AI v1.0.0 • Transparent, Client-First AI Architecture

🛡️ 1. Local-First Client Architecture (Zero Remote Logging)

NasInfinity AI is designed with privacy by default. The host web server (server.js) operates as a zero-dependency static file server. It collects no tracking cookies, no analytics telemetry, and records zero database logs of your conversations, inputs, or personal information.

Your conversation history, user profile memory, and settings are stored locally on your own device using browser localStorage.

🌐 2. Third-Party AI Services & Network Transmission

When you interact with online AI features, your prompt text is transmitted directly and securely over encrypted HTTPS to the service provider selected in your session:

  • Online First AI: Proxied via public text generation endpoints (Pollinations.ai).
  • Google Gemini API: When selected, prompts are sent directly to Google Generative AI servers using your personal API key, subject to the Google Generative AI Terms of Service.
  • OpenAI API: When selected, prompts are sent directly to OpenAI servers using your personal API key, governed by the OpenAI Terms of Use.
  • Wikipedia REST API: Factual summaries are retrieved from Wikimedia Foundation APIs under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA 4.0) license.

🔑 3. API Keys & Credentials Safety

If you provide a Google Gemini or OpenAI API key, it is saved exclusively in your browser's local sandbox storage. Your key is never transmitted to our server, third-party loggers, or unauthorized intermediaries.

🧹 4. User Control & Instant Data Erasure

You hold complete ownership and control over your data. You can erase all chat history and cached memories instantly at any time by clicking "Clear all chats" in the sidebar, or by asking the assistant to "Forget my name" / "Reset memory".

⚖️ 5. Commercial Warranty & Liability Disclaimer

Provided "AS IS": NasInfinity AI and its AI outputs are provided for informational, developer, and educational purposes without warranties of any kind, express or implied.

AI models may occasionally generate inaccurate, incomplete, or biased information. NasInfinity AI is not a certified provider of medical, legal, financial, or safety-critical counsel. Always verify critical code and data prior to production deployment.
